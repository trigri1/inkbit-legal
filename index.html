FAQ

A few practical details before you install.

What is InkBit?

InkBit is a calm journal and reading app built around one daily loop: one passage, one brief note, and one private place to return later.

Is InkBit available on iPhone?

Not yet. Android is available now, and the iPhone version is planned next.

Does InkBit require an account?

No. You can use InkBit without creating an account or sharing personal profile information with the app.

Where are my notes stored?

Notes, favorites, and reflections stay on your device by default. The privacy policy explains analytics, crash reporting, notifications, and the few cases where service providers are involved.
