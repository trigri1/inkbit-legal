FAQ

Simple answers for a quiet app.

What is InkBit?

InkBit is a private reading reflection app for keeping meaningful excerpts, quotes, poems, and small thoughts in one calm literary space.

Is InkBit a journaling app?

InkBit can be used as a journal, but it is shaped around reading and memory rather than long daily entries, social sharing, or productivity tracking.

Can I save quotes and passages?

Yes. InkBit is made for saving loved passages, quotes, poems, excerpts, and the reflections that grow around them.

Is my writing private?

InkBit is designed around private reflection. Journal entries, favorites, reflections, and preferences are stored locally on your device unless you choose to share them.

Does InkBit use streaks or habit pressure?

No. InkBit is a quiet space for one thought at a time, without social feeds, public performance, or pressure to keep a streak.

Who is InkBit for?

InkBit is for readers, journal keepers, students, writers, and anyone who wants a calm private thought journal for words worth returning to.

Is InkBit available on iPhone and Android?

Yes. InkBit is available on the App Store for iPhone and on Google Play for Android devices.
